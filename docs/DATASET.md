# Dataset Documentation

## 1. PlantVillage
- Description: 54,303 lab-controlled images with plain backgrounds.
- Classes: 38 classes across 14 crops.
- Usage: Base training for M1 and M2.

## 2. AgroLeaf AI v3
- Description: ~6.3 GB of high-quality field images.
- Classes: 30+ crops, 120+ diseases.
- Usage: Expanded the M1 and M2 to 34 crops.

## 3. PlantDoc
- Description: Real-world images captured by farmers in the field.
- Classes: 13 crops.
- Usage: Fine-tuning M1 to overcome background bias (Robust M1).
